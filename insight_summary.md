# Samsung Galaxy — Consumer Review Insight Summary

**Data:** 492 unique Samsung Galaxy customer reviews (Amazon.in), across 6 models (duplicates across colour/RAM variants removed)
**Method:** Sentiment classified by star rating (1-2★ Negative, 3★ Neutral, 4-5★ Positive); themes extracted via keyword/phrase frequency analysis

## Key Findings

1. Sentiment is nearly split — 46% positive vs. 45% negative — indicating a polarized customer base rather than uniform satisfaction.
2. Camera quality and battery life are the two most-discussed features in BOTH positive and negative reviews, meaning they are the biggest expectation-setters: when they meet expectations they drive praise, and when they don't, they drive complaints.
3. About 49% of negative reviews reference return, replacement, or delivery language — suggesting a meaningful share of dissatisfaction stems from the post-purchase/logistics experience, not the device itself.
4. "Customer care" and "heating issue" appear as distinct, recurring complaint clusters, alongside strong "don't buy" / "waste of money" language — specific enough to route to product and support teams respectively.

## Recommendation
Given camera/battery drive both praise and complaints, standardizing pre-sale expectation-setting (accurate spec messaging) alongside return-process improvements could meaningfully shift the negative-to-positive ratio without requiring a hardware change.
